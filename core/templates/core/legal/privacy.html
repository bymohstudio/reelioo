Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 15, 2026

1. Overview

Reelioo (“Reelioo”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard information when you access or use the Reelioo platform.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum data necessary to operate the platform and validate licenses.

2.1 Identity & Account Data
  • Username / Handle
  • License Key (via Gumroad)
  • Email address (for account recovery and alerts)
2.2 Technical & Security Data
  • IP address
  • Device and session identifiers
  • Access logs for fraud prevention and system integrity

3. Purpose of Data Processing

We process your data to:

  • Provide access to the Terminal via License verification
  • Secure user accounts and prevent concurrent session abuse
  • Send requested alerts (e.g., Discord/Email notifications)

4. Algorithmic Data Processing

Reelioo uses proprietary deterministic algorithms to process public market data.

  • All market analysis is performed on our internal servers using mathematical physics models.
  • We do not share your personal data, trading history, or journal entries with third-party AI models, LLMs, or data aggregators.
  • Your trading journal data remains private to your account.

5. Payments & Financial Information

Reelioo does not store credit card or banking details.

  • All payments and subscriptions are processed securely via Gumroad.
  • We only retain the License Key and Subscription Status provided by Gumroad API to grant access.
