PROXY: VOLUME PROFILE

VECTOR B

Velocity (v)

PROXY: ORDER VELOCITY

VECTOR C

PROXY: LIMIT WALLS

Every move faces resistance. Friction is the "thickness" of the order book ahead. We calculate the Path of Least Resistance. Price flows where Friction is lowest.
