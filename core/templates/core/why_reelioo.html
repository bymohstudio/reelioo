01. The Core Idea

One indicator is noisy. Two is contradictory. But when five or more independent signals point the same direction at the same time, something real is happening. That's confluence.

Reelioo runs 7 analysis layers on every scan. Each layer votes independently — bullish, bearish, or neutral. The engine sums the votes into a confluence score. Only when the score crosses a threshold does it produce a signal.

LAYER 1

Trend Structure

Is price above or below its moving averages? We use a fast EMA (34-period) and slow EMA (100-period). When both align — and price is above both — the structure is bullish.

LAYER 2

Momentum

We track momentum at two speeds — a fast reactive signal and a slow confirming one. Price-normalized by ATR so it works across any coin. Both must agree for credit.

LAYER 3

Volume

Is volume expanding or contracting? We calculate volume z-scores and flag climax events (2+ standard deviations). A move without volume is a move without conviction.

LAYER 4

Divergence

Price makes new lows but momentum doesn't? Classic bullish divergence. We check this over both 5-bar and 10-bar windows for robustness.

LAYER 5

Order Book

We pull real-time bid/ask depth from Binance Futures and compute the Order Book Imbalance (OBI). Heavy bids with light asks? Buying pressure is building.

LAYER 6

Trend Consistency

Are recent candles consistently closing in one direction? 4 out of 5 bars closing up is a stronger signal than one big green candle.

LAYER 7 · DEFENSE

Trap Detection

Long wicks reveal rejection. If a candle's wick is over 65% of its range, the engine flags it as a potential trap and penalizes the score in that direction. This prevents entering right before a reversal.

02. The Scoring System

Each layer contributes a weighted vote. Structure can give up to 1.0 points. Momentum up to 1.0. Volume up to 0.6. Divergence up to 1.0. And so on. Penalties subtract — a trap candle costs -0.5.

The threshold for an actionable signal is 2.0 for intraday (on 15-minute candles). This means at least 3-4 layers must actively agree. A single indicator spike cannot trigger a signal on its own.

The Silence Protocol

When the score is below threshold, the terminal says HOLD. This isn't a bug — it's the most important feature.

Most signal services push 5-10 signals a day because "more signals = more value." That's wrong. More bad signals = more losses. Reelioo only speaks when the confluence is real.

03. What We Don't Do

We don't use neural networks. We don't predict the future. We don't have access to proprietary exchange data or dark pools. We don't guarantee profits.

Reelioo is a decision-support tool built on publicly available Binance data. It helps you cut through noise and focus on setups where multiple technical signals agree. The rest is still up to your risk management and discipline.
