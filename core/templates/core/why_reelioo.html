The Architecture

SYSTEM LOGIC // LAYERS 1-3
01

Efficiency Ratio (Regime)

THE FILTER

Markets are chaotic (inefficient) 80% of the time. Our engine calculates the Efficiency Ratio of price movement to detect "Chop." If the path of price is inefficient (random walk), the engine disables all signals.

"We don't trade the noise. We wait for the signal."

02

Vector Force Analysis

THE DIRECTION

Price is an object in motion. We measure its Velocity (Momentum) and Mass (Volume). Using standard deviation Z-Scores, we detect when "Whale Mass" is applied to price, confirming that a move is institutional, not retail.

"Volume confirms the truth. Price is just the advertisement."

03

Liquidity Friction

THE TRIGGER

Trends often reverse at liquidity pools. We identify "Traps" and "Sweeps" where retail stops are hunted. The engine aligns entry with these friction points to ensure you are on the side of the liquidity provider, not the victim.

"We buy where others are forced to sell."
