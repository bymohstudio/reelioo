Retail traders operate at a structural disadvantage. Institutional desks utilize high-frequency algorithms to detect liquidity vacuums and order flow imbalances milliseconds before price action occurs. Reelioo bridges this gap.

We do not use subjective technical analysis. Instead, we deploy a 3-Model Ensemble that filters noise, determines bias, and validates momentum before a signal is ever generated.

The Architecture

SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE // LAYERS 1-3
01

Macro-Regime Detection

TIMEFRAME: 4H // THE FILTER

Before analyzing direction, we classify the market state. Our stochastic models measure volatility entropy to determine if the asset is in a Trend State or Mean Reversion (Chop) State.

"If volatility is compressing below the historic mean, the system remains silent to preserve capital."

02

Directional Probability Vectors

TIMEFRAME: 1H // THE BIAS

Once a trend regime is validated, we utilize Non-Linear Decision Trees to analyze Order Flow Imbalance and Momentum Physics. This layer calculates the raw probability of a Bullish vs. Bearish expansion.

"We track the aggressive vs. passive buying pressure to determine true institutional intent."

03

Micro-Structure Validation

TIMEFRAME: 15M // THE TRIGGER

The final check. We analyze volume velocity on lower timeframes to ensure the breakout has sufficient liquidity backing. This prevents "fakeouts" by requiring a significant deviation in volume Z-scores.

"A signal is only released if immediate execution conditions are mathematically favorable."
