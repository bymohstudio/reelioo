THE SUPREME ENGINE

01 // Whale Z-Score

We calculate the Standard Deviation (Z-Score) of volume anomalies. A Z-Score > 2.0 indicates institutional accumulation that retail traders typically miss. This alerts us to "Smart Money" entering before price moves.

02 // Volatility Squeeze

Markets move in cycles of expansion and contraction. Our engine detects Bollinger Band Squeezes (Low Volatility). We enter when the market is quiet, positioning you *before* the violent expansion phase.

03 // AI Synthesis

Our XGBoost models process historical price action to generate a probability score (0-100%). This is combined with real-time news sentiment analysis to confirm the technical setup.
